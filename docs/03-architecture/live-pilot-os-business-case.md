# LivePilot OS Business Case

> **Status:** Hypothesis brief. The Business Model Canvas is a source of hypotheses, not proof of market demand or validated unit economics.

## Opportunity hypothesis

Live-commerce brands and studios may need a neutral operations layer that combines Host guidance with controlled back-office actions across live-commerce channels. The proposed value is reducing operational misses and preventing unsafe promotion or inventory actions while preserving the Host's selling rhythm.

## Customer and user hypotheses

| Hypothesis | Source | Validation still needed |
|---|---|---|
| Hosts need brief cues grounded in approved product facts. | Core Features; Business Model Canvas | Observe Hosts during real sessions and measure cue usefulness/distraction. |
| Live Ops teams need a shared view of chat intent, offers, and action history. | Feature Map; Business Model Canvas | Interview and workflow observation with studio operators. |
| Brands may avoid sharing COGS with marketplaces and prefer a separate margin control. | Business Model Canvas | Validate with sellers and review actual current workflows. |
| Studios/MCNs may be an effective distribution and buyer segment. | Business Model Canvas | Confirm buyer, budget owner, procurement path, and deployment needs. |

## Value hypotheses

- Reduce time from repeated audience question to a usable Host cue.
- Prevent below-floor offers from being issued.
- Reduce overselling and duplicate actions through controlled adapters and reservations.
- Make automated decisions reviewable by the operator.

## Commercial model hypotheses

The canvas proposes studio-room SaaS tiers, broadcast-hour packs, and enterprise deployment. These are unvalidated options; no price, margin, forecast, or customer count is approved by this document.

## Evidence needed

- Customer interviews and observed live-session workflows.
- Baseline rates for missed questions, offer mistakes, overselling, and operator workload.
- Willingness-to-pay evidence from the buyer, not only Host interest.
- Platform integration and certification feasibility.
- Cost-to-serve estimates for inference, real-time transport, and support.

## Decision gate

Do not use canvas estimates such as assistant salary comparisons or ROI payback as factual pitch claims until their source, date, assumptions, and validation are recorded. Promote business hypotheses only after evidence is captured in the project knowledge map.
