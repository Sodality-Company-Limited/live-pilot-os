## Commercial Unit Economics & Defensibility Highlights

- **ROI Payback Anchor:** Compared against a full-time Bangkok studio operations assistant (~฿18,000–฿25,000/month per shift), LivePilot OS is priced at a fraction of a single ops seat. Preventing just two negative-margin flash-sale mistakes per month pays for the subscription before conversion gains are counted.
- **Why Brands Buy Over Native Marketplace Tools:**
  1. **COGS Confidentiality:** Brands will not upload true factory COGS and supplier margins into Shopee or TikTok seller dashboards; LivePilot keeps unit costs isolated on the merchant's server.
  2. **Cross-Platform Stock Neutrality:** Sellers streaming simultaneously on Shopee Live, TikTok Shop, and Lazada need a neutral inventory reservation layer that prevents overselling across competing platforms.
  3. **Creator vs. Brand Alignment:** Affiliate creators earn a percentage of GMV and push for deep discounts, whereas brand owners require contribution margin protection. LivePilot enables high-energy creator conversion within strict brand margin guardrails.
