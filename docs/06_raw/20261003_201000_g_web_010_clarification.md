## 1. Context & Business Intent

In production-grade enterprise software and cognitive agent operating systems, unverified media assets pose grave intellectual property, security, accessibility, and performance hazards:
1. **IP & Licensing Liabilities:** Ad-hoc icons, images, or audio snippets borrowed without explicit license verification risk copyright infringement and legal disqualification.
2. **Performance Debt & Layout Shifts:** Bloated, uncompressed bitmaps (>500KB) inflate initial bundle transfer sizes, degrade Largest Contentful Paint (LCP), and distort responsive layouts if missing explicit aspect ratios.
3. **Auditory Intrusiveness & Cognitive Strain:** Unregulated sound feedback with sharp transients, digital clipping, or excessive durations (>500ms) alienates users, causes cognitive fatigue, and violates accessibility standards if mute suppression is not universally controllable.

Goal `G-WEB-010` codifies and programmatically enforces a cryptographic asset and reference provenance ledger for Soda OS that guarantees:
- **Modern Visual Compression:** All visual assets are strictly codified in modern vector (SVG) or compressed (WebP) formats with a mandatory $< 250\text{KB}$ ceiling.
- **Aspect Ratio & Accessibility Preservation:** SVGs require `viewBox` attributes for responsive scaling and `<title>` / `aria-label` metadata for screen reader assistive technologies.
- **Earcon Acoustic Integrity:** All audio assets are restricted to micro-feedback earcons with duration $\le 500\text{ms}$, normalized to $-16\text{ to } -18\text{ LUFS}$ / dBFS, with zero digital clipping and accessible mute controls.
- **Cryptographic Provenance:** Every asset is cataloged with an immutable SHA-256 digest in `docs/02-product/design/asset-provenance-manifest.json` tied to a verified licensing authority.
