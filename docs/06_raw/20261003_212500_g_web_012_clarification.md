## 1. Context & Business Intent

In complex multi-agent cognitive systems, closing the feedback loop between execution outcomes and model training is paramount to prevent permanent drift:
1. **Uncalibrated Model Degradation:** Without strictly proper scoring, models optimize for overconfident assertions rather than calibrated epistemic uncertainty.
2. **Loss of Empirical Trajectory Evidence:** If execution outcomes (pixel diff ratios, test failures, human corrections, contrast violations) are discarded upon goal closeout, subsequent agent generations repeat identical mistakes.
3. **Fragmented Quality Gates:** Relying on ad-hoc or piecemeal checks rather than a unified battery of specialized domain harnesses leads to stealth regressions in production.

Goal `G-WEB-012` addresses these imperatives by:
- Executing all 9 specialized DAVE verification harnesses across decision schemas, references, traces, UI specs, tokens, visual regressions, motion physics, visual formats, and acoustic earcons.
- Computing strictly proper logarithmic scores ($R = y \ln(c) + (1-y)\ln(1-c)$) across all recorded decisions, with an asymmetric 10x penalty ($c^2 \times 10.0$) targeting confident false acceptances.
- Exporting a structured, partitioned (80% train, 10% validation, 10% test) RLCD training dataset to `artifacts/rlcd/train_dataset.jsonl` for continuous downstream fine-tuning.
