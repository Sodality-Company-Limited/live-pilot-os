# EPIC: PROMO

**Slug:** PROMO  
**Name:** Promotion Service  
**Owner:** Engineer 2

## Intent

Deliver the first production-shaped LivePilot microservice from contract and
state ownership through persistence, messaging, reliability, observability,
and DigitalOcean deployment readiness. Promotion service owns the
Promotion/Voucher lifecycle and remains independently deployable while using
versioned contracts with margin, inventory, approval/control, execution, and
audit services.

## Out of this epic

- Identity/session, live-session, telemetry, intent/risk, catalog, and JEV/Copilot services owned by Engineer 1.
- Margin calculation, inventory reservation, approval/control, execution/adapter safety, and audit remain separate service boundaries even when Engineer 2 coordinates their contracts.
- Production marketplace mutation, real provider credentials, and production deployment apply require the applicable approval and release gates.
