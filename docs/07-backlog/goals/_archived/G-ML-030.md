## Context
Ingests raw multi-CLI execution trajectories from `.agents/telemetry/trajectories/` (established in G-ML-029) and synthesizes structured RLCD (Reinforcement Learning from Compiler/Execution Data) contrastive preference pairs $(y_w, y_l)$ to train DAVE Decision/Action routing and EBM energy landscapes. Grounded in [`scripts/lib/dave-trajectory-builder.mjs`](file:///scripts/lib/dave-trajectory-builder.mjs) and [`docs/06_raw/20261023_165354_liquid_ebm_jepa_and_dave_trajectory_engine_blueprint.md`](file:///docs/06_raw/20261023_165354_liquid_ebm_jepa_and_dave_trajectory_engine_blueprint.md).

## Intent
**Why:** Continuous-time EBMs and DAVE require contrastive winning/losing decision pairs where winning actions ($y_w$) satisfy verification tests and losing actions ($y_l$) represent failed runs, syntax errors, or touch map violations.  
**Done when:** `scripts/lib/dave-trajectory-builder.mjs` consumes trajectory records, maps verification and test results to asymmetric rewards, and exports partitioned JSONL datasets to `artifacts/rlcd/train.jsonl` and `artifacts/rlcd/val.jsonl` with zero data leakage.  
**Unblocks:** G-ML-031, G-ML-032.  

## In
- Trajectory ingestion and contrastive pairing in `scripts/lib/dave-trajectory-builder.mjs`.
- Preference record schema validation against `schemas/dave-preference-pair.schema.json`.
- Deterministic train/val/test partitioning via cryptographic SHA-256 seed.
- Unit and property tests in `tests/ml/test-trajectory-rlcd-builder.mjs`.

## Out
- Neural network weight optimization (handled in G-ML-031 and G-ML-032).
- Real-time online model inference (handled in DAVE runtime).

## Work steps
### Step 1 — Trajectory Pair Transformer & Empirical Verification Outcome Matcher
**Allowed files:** `scripts/lib/dave-trajectory-builder.mjs`, `schemas/dave-preference-pair.schema.json`  
**Actions:**
1. Ingest raw execution trajectories from `.agents/telemetry/trajectories/`.
2. Pair successful CLI runs with failed or rejected alternatives across identical or similar task mandates.
**Verification command:** `node --test tests/ml/test-trajectory-rlcd-builder.mjs`  
**Completion gate:** 100% green test verifying paired extraction of winning and losing trajectories.  
**Stop condition:** Unpaired orphaned records without contrastive counterparts.  
**Zero-Mock invariant check:** 100% operational implementation; zero stubs, zero mocks, zero placeholders.

### Step 2 — Calibrated Reward Engine with Asymmetric Risk Penalty
**Allowed files:** `scripts/lib/dave-trajectory-builder.mjs`  
**Actions:**
1. Assign positive rewards for green tests and minimal token/latency footprint.
2. Apply 10x asymmetric penalty for security boundaries, invariant violations, or touch-map leaks.
**Verification command:** `node --test tests/ml/test-trajectory-rlcd-builder.mjs`  
**Completion gate:** Reward bounds verified strictly within $[-1.0, 1.0]$.  
**Stop condition:** Reward overflow or inverted reward sign on failed verification.  
**Zero-Mock invariant check:** 100% operational implementation; zero stubs, zero mocks, zero placeholders.

### Step 3 — Train/Validation/Test Split Partitioner with Zero-Leak Invariant
**Allowed files:** `scripts/lib/dave-trajectory-builder.mjs`, `artifacts/rlcd/`  
**Actions:**
1. Partition pairs 80/10/10 by task hash to guarantee zero cross-contamination.
2. Export partitioned JSONL files with physical SHA-256 data digests.
**Verification command:** `node --test tests/ml/test-trajectory-rlcd-builder.mjs`  
**Completion gate:** Hash disjointness between train, validation, and test datasets verified.  
**Stop condition:** Data overlap between partitions.  
**Zero-Mock invariant check:** 100% operational implementation; zero stubs, zero mocks, zero placeholders.

## Test plan
- Commands from `AGENTS.md` **Standard commands**

### 1. Positive Tests
- Verify successful pairing of verified and unverified runs for identical tasks.
### 2. Negative Tests
- Verify rejection of records with corrupt JSON or invalid score ranges.
### 3. Fail-Closed & Chaos Tests
- Verify pipeline halts safely if source trajectory directory is empty or missing.
### 4. Temporal Causality Tests ($t \le T$)
- Verify training pairs preserve temporal causality; future runs do not pollute historical baseline.
### 5. Security & Air-Gap Checks
- Verify zero external network requests during dataset synthesis.
### 6. Invariants & System Laws Verification
- Verify partition completeness: $|D_{\text{train}}| + |D_{\text{val}}| + |D_{\text{test}}| = |D_{\text{total}}|$.
### 7. Formal Verification & Solvers
- Verify mathematical monotonicity of reward ranking: $y_w \succ y_l \iff R(y_w) > R(y_l)$.
### 8. Harness Engineering & Modeling
- Verify integration with `scripts/harness/verification-orchestrator-harness.mjs`.
### 9. Adversarial Stress & Edge Pathologies
- Verify degenerate dataset handling (all wins or all losses) without crashing.
### 10. Concurrency, Race Conditions & Memory Models
- Verify atomic output file writing preventing partial or truncated datasets.
### 11. Advanced Testing Paradigms & Mutation Testing
- Verify mutation test kill threshold $\ge 85\%$ across reward scoring algorithms.
