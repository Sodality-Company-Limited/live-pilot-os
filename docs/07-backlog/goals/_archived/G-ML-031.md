**Completion gate:** 100% green pytest verifying tensor shapes and split integrity under conda py310.  
**Stop condition:** Tensor dimension mismatch or NaN embeddings.

### Step 2 — Train Contrastive EBM Energy Function Carving Valid Action Basins
**Allowed files:** `scripts/ml/models/ebm_action_landscape.py`, `scripts/ml/train_ebm_jepa_distillation.py`  
**Actions:**
1. Implement energy network $E(s, a)$ computing scalar compatibility.
2. Train with margin contrastive loss driving valid trajectories to $E \to 0$ and perturbations to $E \ge M$.
**Verification command:** `pytest tests/ml/test_ebm_jepa_training.py -k test_ebm_convergence`  
**Completion gate:** Training loss reaches convergence with negative energy slope for valid actions.  
**Stop condition:** Mode collapse or flat energy landscape.  
**Zero-Mock invariant check:** 100% operational implementation; zero stubs, zero mocks, zero placeholders.

### Step 3 — Evaluate Latent Prediction Error and Energy Margin Discrimination
**Allowed files:** `scripts/ml/eval_ebm_jepa.py`, `artifacts/checkpoints/`  
**Actions:**
1. Evaluate JEPA latent predictor error against held-out test splits.
2. Export serialized TorchScript model checkpoints with cryptographic SHA-256 manifests.
**Verification command:** `pytest tests/ml/test_ebm_jepa_training.py -k test_evaluation_metrics`  
**Completion gate:** Validation margin contrast $\ge 2.0$; JEPA latent cosine similarity $\ge 0.88$.  
**Stop condition:** Metric thresholds not met.
