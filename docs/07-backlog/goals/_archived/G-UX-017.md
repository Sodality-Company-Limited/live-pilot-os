## Context

Standard generative models suffer from severe overconfidence on ambiguous, edge-case, or adversarial inputs. Calibration-Aware Reinforcement Learning (CARL) directly couples policy optimization with strictly proper scoring rules, penalizes confident mistakes asymmetrically, and rewards models for expressing justified doubt.

## Intent

Equip generative policies with dual-head decision and confidence predictions calibrated to proper epistemic probabilities while rewarding justified doubt.

**Why:** Grounding model autonomy in mathematically proven calibration metrics guarantees fail-closed safety by preventing overconfident hallucinations on edge cases.  
**Done when:** Dual-head CARL loss and engine are operational, strictly proper Brier scoring is enforced, 10x overconfidence penalties trigger on incorrect high-confidence claims, doubt is rewarded on ambiguous inputs, and statistical invariants (Brier <= 0.15, ECE <= 0.05, AUROC >= 0.88, HCER <= 0.010) are proven.  
**Unblocks:** G-UX-018 (Multi-Agent LoRA Adapters), G-UX-019 (Model Provenance Ledger).

## How

Implement PyTorch CARL loss module in `code/ml/src/training/carl_loss.py` and Node.js calibration engine in `scripts/lib/carl-engine.mjs`. Construct dual-head architecture: decision generation head alongside confidence estimation head $c \in [0.0, 1.0]$. Formulate composite objective combining task cross-entropy $\mathcal{L}_{\text{task}}$, strictly proper Brier score $\frac{1}{N} \sum (c_i - y_i^*)^2$, asymmetric $10\times$ overconfidence penalty $\mathcal{P}_{\text{overconf}} = \mathbb{I}(y^* = 0) \cdot \mathbb{I}(c \ge 0.75) \cdot 10.0 \cdot c^2$, and doubt reward $+1.0 \cdot (1-c)$ on deferral routes. Verify with unit tests and `scripts/harness/carl-engine-harness.mjs`.
