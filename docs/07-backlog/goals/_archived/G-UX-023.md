## Context

Deceptive design patterns (confirmshaming, hidden subscriptions, fake urgency, roach motels) and unredacted PII exposure violate consumer protection laws (FTC, European Digital Services Act, GDPR, PDPA). Autonomous design generation models must be constrained by an adversarial safety gate that rejects deceptive proposals fail-closed before human exposure or production generation.

## Intent

Implement an adversarial safety gate rejecting deceptive dark patterns and unredacted PII via fail-closed routing.

**Why:** Deceptive dark patterns and PII exposure harm users and violate consumer protection regulations; autonomous systems must fail closed when deceptive designs are detected.  
**Done when:** Safety gate evaluates all 14 canonical deceptive dark pattern classes, detects unredacted PII with Luhn card validation, evaluates 50-probe adversarial battery, and routes decisions fail closed (`REJECT_AS_UNSAFE`, `DEFER`, `PROCEED`).  
**Unblocks:** Production deployment of the complete Soda OS Multimodal UX Decision Layer.

## How

Implement `scripts/lib/dark-pattern-safety-gate.mjs` codifying detectors for all 14 canonical dark pattern categories: confirmshaming, hidden subscription, urgency/false scarcity, trick questions, sneaking, roach motel, price comparison prevention, bait and switch, disguised ads, misdirection, social proof fabrication, default preselection, nagging, and forced action. Implement Luhn mod 10 checksum validation:
$$\sum_{i=1}^{n} d'_i \equiv 0 \pmod{10}$$
Detect unredacted PII (emails, phone numbers, SSNs, credit cards, secret API keys). Curate a 50-probe adversarial battery and enforce fail-closed routing actions: dark pattern or PII exposure assigns confidence $0.000$ and routes to `REJECT_AS_UNSAFE`; underspecified prompts route to `DEFER`; clean enterprise specifications route to `PROCEED`. Build bitwise Python parity in `code/ml/src/eval/dark_pattern_safety_gate.py`. Verify across dual runtimes and enforce safety gate thresholds via `scripts/harness/dark-pattern-safety-gate-harness.mjs`.
