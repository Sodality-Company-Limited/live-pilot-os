## Context

Autonomous AI design agents risk hallucinations, overconfidence, and opacity without structured Human-in-the-Loop review mechanisms. Soda OS addresses this by generating compact, zero-opacity Markdown review packets that present spatial zone deconstructions, calibrated confidence metrics, and interactive approval actions. Furthermore, human corrections are parsed across a 9-class taxonomy and serialized into paired preference tuples for continuous RLCD learning.

## Intent

Formats zero-opacity markdown review packets, parses 9-class correction taxonomy, and serializes RLCD preference tuples.

**Why:** Provides auditable human approval gates and continuously trains downstream alignment policies with structured human corrections.  
**Done when:** Review packets format all mandatory governance sections, taxonomy parser achieves 100% accuracy on feedback corpus, and RLCD serializer outputs valid preference pairs with 10x ethical penalties.  
**Unblocks:** Closed-loop human learning and governance review across Soda OS.

## How

Implement `scripts/lib/hitl-review-packet.mjs` providing `formatReviewPacket`, `parseHumanCorrection`, and `serializeRlcdPreferencePair`. Codify the 9 canonical correction taxonomy categories (layout, typography, color, navigation, motion, component_state, form_input, feedback_modal, ethical_guardrail). Support interactive markdown checkboxes (`[Approve]`, `[Reject]`, `[Request Revision]`, `[Approve with Conditions]`). Extract targeted zones, directives, and sentiment from freeform text. Serialize preference pairs into DPO/RLCD format with 10.0x loss weight for ethical guardrails. Verify via unit tests in `tests/unit/test-hitl-review-packet.mjs` and domain harness `scripts/harness/hitl-review-packet-harness.mjs`.
